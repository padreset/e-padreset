E-Pad Reset

Unofficial Android USB OTG utility for compatible Epson printers. Read printer status, inspect ink and waste counters, and reset waste ink counters directly from your device.

Epson inkjet printers only. The app does not work with printers from other brands, and it cannot reset a maintenance box that carries its own chip.

Missing model? Use “Help add this model” in the app to review a limited report before sharing. Support needs verified model data and hardware tests and is not guaranteed. The app has no Internet permission; your browser or chosen sharing app handles any external communication. Do not publish raw logs, printer serial numbers or EEPROM backups.
